About

A school, a Scopus-indexed journal, an international accreditation and a publishing house. I build things in higher education that did not exist before, and I make them work.

At Sricity International University I designed and launched the B.Tech. in Smart Manufacturing, a work-integrated dual program in which students spend over 30 months in industry. The first cohort began classes in September 2026.

Over fourteen years I have taught and led programs at six campuses in India, the UAE and Malaysia, with a steady focus on curriculum reform, outcome-based education and accreditation. I also founded the Scopus-indexed Journal of Computers, Mechanical and Management and serve as its Editor-in-Chief.

What I Have Built

Built a school before its first student arrived

Designed the School of Advanced Manufacturing at SIU from a blank page: a 160-credit dual program with over 30 months in industry, two industry tracks, labs and faculty. The first cohort has been in class since September 2026.

Took a journal from launch to Scopus

Founded the Journal of Computers, Mechanical and Management in 2022 and, as Editor-in-Chief, led it into Scopus.

Won full-term accreditation abroad

Secured full-term undergraduate accreditation from EAC Malaysia, a Washington Accord signatory, for Manipal International University. Then ran accreditation for B.Tech. Industrial Engineering at MIT Manipal for six years.

Teaches the teachers

Globally certified OBE trainer. Engineering colleges bring me in to run NBA and OBE workshops for their faculty, and one has placed me on its department advisory board.

Turned editorial skill into a business

Founded SHORAM Editorial Services in 2026, a registered academic publishing house and Crossref member that publishes conference proceedings for Karnataka institutions.

Trusted with strategy

Deputy Project Management Officer for strategic development, and Head of Mechatronics, at Rajalakshmi Engineering College, Chennai.
